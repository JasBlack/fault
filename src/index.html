FAULT / FIELD NOTES

A picture is a signal.
Make it misbehave.

Load a video or image, or start with the internal patterns. Pick a patch, then change Frequency and Resonance. Push Drive and Fold to make edges ring and colours break apart.

Three places to intervene

Bend: two resonant signal bands, thresholds, nonlinear folding, and delayed signal memory.
Break: mix a second unsynchronised signal, release receiver lock, and bend line timing.
Output: frame feedback, colour, optional scanlines and dry/wet mix.

Signal feedback remembers a displaced waveform from the previous frame. Frame feedback recirculates the decoded picture. Use Clear feedback when a patch runs away.

Crystal display: RGB cell size sets the width of one red/green/blue group in output pixels. RGB light strength controls its visibility; set it to zero for a clean image. Edge sharpness and Source detail recover definition independently of the bend. Small cells are easiest to judge fullscreen.

Record captures the live processed canvas without audio. Stop to get a downloadable take. Actual frame rate depends on your computer. Save patches separately to keep your settings.
