FAULT / FIELD NOTES

A picture is a signal.
Make it misbehave.

Start: press open picture / video or drop a picture, GIF or video anywhere. Pick a look, then tune it in the console on the right. Double-click or right-click a slider to reset it.

The console

Bend: resonant bands that make edges ring, Relief for embossed edges over grey, signal fold and signal memory.
Break: mix in a second signal (the interferer: ＋ clip, bars or none), release the receiver lock, bend lines and roll the picture.
Display: sharpness, the video-synth scan processor and colouriser, frame feedback, colour, and colour cleanup to remove rainbow speckle.
Tube: choose the set the picture is shown on (Sony PVM, Trinitron TV, slot mask, dot mask, mono) and adjust its mask, scanline beam, contrast, white point, glow and glass. Carrier texture adds the herringbone dot pattern.
Mask: put the effect on part of the picture. Key it by brightness or a picked colour, or click the picture with smart select or the colour wand (Alt-click removes). On video the selection follows its subject by colour. Show mask tints the affected area purple.

Capture

Capture saves a picture as a PNG at once. With a video it rewinds, records the clip from start to end and downloads it as an MP4 (WebM in Firefox). Record live, in the capture panel, records from now until you press stop. There is no audio. The output keeps the shape of your source, so 9:16 stays 9:16.

Memory: save patch keeps your settings as a file; load patch (or dropping the file) restores them. Footage is not stored in patches.

Keys: Space pauses, B shows the clean source, C clears signal and frame memory.

Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Smart select downloads a small model the first time it is used. This is an original, simplified model of composite video and a cathode-ray tube, not a circuit-accurate emulation of any device. Start with modest levels if you prefer less flicker.
